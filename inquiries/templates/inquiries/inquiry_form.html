✂️ Atelier Measurement Guide

Standard Fit Diagram

Reference the key anatomical landmarks before noting your figures.

For the most accurate fit, use a soft measuring tape and ask someone to assist you. Keep the tape comfortably snug without digging in.

Upper Body (Shirts & Jackets)
Chest

Wrap the tape under your armpits, around the fullest part of your chest and shoulder blades. Keep the tape level across your back.

Shoulder Width

Measure across the top of the back from the edge of one shoulder tip to the edge of the other shoulder tip.

Sleeve Length

Start the tape at the shoulder seam and measure down the outside of the arm to just past the wrist bone.

Lower Body (Trousers)
Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, where your dress trousers sit. Insert one finger between the tape and your body for comfort.

Inseam

Measure from the lowest part of the crotch seam straight down the inside of the leg to your preferred hem length.

Hips / Seat

Stand with your heels together and measure around the fullest point of your hips and seat.
